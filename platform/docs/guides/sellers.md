# Seller guide

## 1. Get verified

Sign in, open **Account → Verification** (`/account/verification`), choose **Seller**, and upload clear
photos of your National ID, passport or driving licence (a trade licence or TIN certificate helps too). Documents are stored privately and
only staff reviewing your request can open them; your ID number is never shown. You'll get a notification
when staff approve the request, or ask you to resubmit with a reason.

## 2. Set up your store

**Seller → Store** (`/seller/store`): store name, logo and banner, description, district, phone, and your
return and shipping policies. Readers see your store at `/stores/<your-store>` with its rating.

## 3. List books and products

**Seller → Listings** (`/seller/products`):

- **Books:** enter the ISBN to match the book in the catalogue, then add your offer — condition (new, used
  like new / good / acceptable, with a note about any marks), price, an optional compare-at price, and stock.
  Your offer appears on the book's page next to other sellers'.
- **Other products** (stationery, educational items): title, category, photos (up to 8), and variants such
  as colour or size, each with its own price and stock.
- Stock changes are recorded in a ledger, so you can always see why a number changed.

Only sell books you have the right to sell. Pirated copies and unauthorised photocopies are removed and
can get the store suspended.

## 4. Handle orders

**Seller → Orders** (`/seller/orders`) lists your parcels. A reader's order with books from several sellers
is split, so you only see and ship your part.

1. **Confirm** the order (or cancel with a reason if you can't fulfil it — the reader is refunded).
2. **Processing** while you pack it.
3. **Shipped:** choose the courier and add the tracking number. The reader gets a notification with
   tracking.
4. **Out for delivery** and **Delivered** as the courier reports.

Returns requested within the return window appear under **Returns**: approve and arrange the pickup, then
mark the return received to trigger the refund.

## 5. Get paid

**Seller → Payouts** (`/seller/payouts`) shows your balance: delivered sales minus the platform commission
and any refunds. Add the account where you want to be paid there (bKash, Nagad, Rocket or bank). Staff pay
your balance to that account and record the reference; you get a notification for
each payout.

## Tips

- Keep stock accurate: an offer at zero stock shows as out of stock and can't be added to carts.
- Describe used books honestly. Good condition notes mean fewer returns and better ratings.
