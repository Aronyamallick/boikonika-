# Author and publisher guide

## Get verified

Open **Account → Verification**, choose **Author** or **Publisher**, and upload your identity document
(publishers also add a trade licence or publishing document). Verified authors and publishers get a badge.

## Your author page and dashboard

Most authors already have a page from the catalogue. In the **Author dashboard** (`/author`), search for
your name under **Claim your author page** and send a claim with a short message and evidence (a link to
your publisher's page, an interview, a book's copyright page). Staff check it against your verification
and connect the page to your account. From the dashboard you can then:

- edit your bio and birth year (your name comes from your verification);
- see your books with their ratings and reviews;
- post a short update to your followers (a few a day) — it arrives as a notification, optionally linked to
  one of your books, and readers can turn author updates off.

## Publisher dashboard

The **Publisher dashboard** (`/publisher`) manages your publishing house's page: logo, description and
founding year, the books linked to it, and the authors you publish. The registered name comes from your
verification; contact support to change it.

## E-books (Studio)

**Studio → Digital** (`/studio/digital`) lets rights holders sell or give away e-books:

1. **Upload** a PDF or EPUB (up to 50 MB) and confirm the rights declaration — that you are the author or
   publisher and hold the digital rights. The declaration is stored with the file.
2. **Offer it** free or at a price. Selling needs an approved seller account (see the seller guide); paid
   e-books are paid out like any seller sale. Free e-books need no seller account.
3. **Previews:** add a sample chapter, sample pages or let readers see the first part of the PDF (up to the
   preview limit set by the platform).

Readers who buy or claim the e-book download it from **My Library** through short-lived links; downloads per
day are limited. Replacing the file keeps buyers' access. If a copyright complaint is upheld the e-book is
taken down; you can send a counter-notice from your account standing page.

## Rules

Only upload books you hold the rights to. Don't use AI to imitate another living author's voice or to
pass off generated text as theirs. Quotes in posts must be short passages, never reproductions of the
book.
